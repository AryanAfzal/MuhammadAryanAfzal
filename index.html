Featured Project Release • AutoGrade

Revolutionizing Exam Grading with TrOCR & AI: Building AutoGrade

Thrilled to present AutoGrade, an end-to-end AI-powered handwritten exam paper evaluation platform designed to eliminate grading bottlenecks while ensuring transparency and fairness!

How it works: Students or evaluators upload handwritten exam sheets. Our system uses a vision transformer (TrOCR Base) to recognize complex cursive handwriting with 98% confidence, segments individual answers per question, and cross-references them against an evaluation rubric with automated grading and detailed feedback.

Architecture: Next.js 14 frontend, NestJS API gateway, PyTorch OCR microservice, and PostgreSQL database.
